title: "Piranesi"
author: Susanna Clarke
date: 2026-08-27
categories: fiction
rating: 4/5
---

Piranesi is my favorite kind of story—one where you slowly discover what is really going on. While I don't want to share too much more detail, I will say that it's difficult to categorize. It's more "fantastical" than "fantasy" (kind of like "The Little Prince" or "Flatland" in some ways). I'm not sure how I feel about the ending but overall it was really enjoyable.
