I checked out this book specifically because the title sounded intriguing. Once I started reading, I realized that it was targeted at full-time entrepreneurs with active businesses, digging into tactical steps they could take to free up more time. The book was probably ok, but I just wasn't in that space in my life when I started reading it, so I noped out after one chapter.
