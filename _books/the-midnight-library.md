---
title: "The Midnight Library"
author: Matt Haig
date: 2026-10-26
categories: fiction
rating: 4/5
---

This fictional story follows Nora Seed, a woman from England who has a near-death experience and finds herself in a library full of books containing the written stories of alternate versions of her life. As the story progresses, she is given opportunities to live portions of these alternate lives—an experience that gives her perspective. The book had several themes, among them, the idea that life is a privilege (that we often take for granted), and that we seldom recognize the impact our decisions can have on others. Some aspects of the storyline were a bit contrived, but I decide not to care too much. I'm a sucker for storylines where a person gets multiple chances at living their life (see [Replay](https://www.bryanbraun.com/books/#replay) and [Dark Matter](https://www.bryanbraun.com/books/#dark-matter)).
