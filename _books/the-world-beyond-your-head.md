This book might be better named, "the philosophy of distraction." It's a long book, with long words... lots of philosophy and higher-ed speak. I did like the concept of *silence* as a luxury good, existing in the commons, thus becoming vulnerable to encroachment by market forces. Ultimately though, I couldn't persuade myself to work through it and I lost interest.
