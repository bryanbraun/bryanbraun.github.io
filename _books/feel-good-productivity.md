title: "Feel-Good Productivity"
author: Ali Abdaal
date: 2026-08-13
categories: non-fiction
rating: 2/5
---

This is a book about productivity, written from the perspective of a former medical doctor turned productivity entrepreneur. Most of the productivity advice was pretty standard. A common thread throughout the advice was to not always push harder, but to listen to what the resistance might be trying to tell you (which makes sense, given the author's career change). This idea is what drew me to the book, but in practice, I found the reading to be a slog. I don't think it was poorly written—a younger version of me would probably have enjoyed it. But the tips, the studies... I just feel like I've read a lot of this stuff before.
