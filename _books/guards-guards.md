This was a fantasy story from Terry Pratchett's "Discworld" series. I had heard positive things about Pratchett's writing, and Reddit suggested that this book might be a good place to start. The main storyline was about a medieval-ish city trying to deal with a dragon problem. It wasn't *bad*. Pratchett's niche is writing fantasy that pokes fun at fantasy, which intrigued me, but I haven't read much fantasy (and I haven't particularly liked the fantasy that I've read) so I suspect that some of the gags flew over my head. There were some genuinely funny interactions in the secret society, and I liked how the dragons in this story were basically the opposite of the dragon stereotype (most of them were small, sickly, and prone to accidentally exploding). That said, I found the dialog tiring, and was a bit relieved when it was over.
